FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Adhyapak Saathi free to use?
Yes, completely free. Adhyapak Saathi is an open-access platform. There are no subscriptions, no registration requirements, and no hidden costs. All practice tests, exam news, and resources are freely available.
Which examinations does the site cover?
Adhyapak Saathi covers: TAT (Secondary & Higher Secondary), TET I, TET II, UGC NET, GSET, and CTET. Practice tests include Child Development & Pedagogy, Teaching Aptitude, General Knowledge, and Sanskrit — all mapped to official syllabi.
I found an incorrect question or answer. What should I do?
Please use the Suggestion Box on this page and select "Report Error". Mention the subject, the question text, and what you believe the correct answer is. Our team verifies and corrects errors within 24 hours.
How do I share an important exam notification?
Use the Contact Form above and select "Exam News Tip". Include the source (official URL if available) and the key details. If verified, we will publish it on the Exam News page promptly.
