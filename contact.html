    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is Adhyapak Saathi free to use?
    Yes, completely free. Adhyapak Saathi is an open-access platform hosted on GitHub Pages. There are no subscriptions, no registration requirements, and no hidden costs. All practice tests, exam news, and resources are freely available.
    Which examinations does the site cover?
    Adhyapak Saathi covers: TAT (Secondary), TAT (Higher Secondary), TET I, TET II, UGC NET, GSET, and CTET. Practice tests include Child Development & Pedagogy, Teaching Aptitude, Research Aptitude, Language, and Gujarat GK — all mapped to official syllabi.
    I found an incorrect question or answer. What should I do?
    Please use the Suggestion Box on this page and select "Correct a Quiz Answer or Error" as the category. Mention the subject, the question text, and what you believe the correct answer is with reasoning. Our team verifies and corrects errors within 48 hours.
    How do I share an important exam notification?
    Use the Contact Form above and select "Exam News Tip" as the message type. Include the source (official URL if available), the exam name, and the key details. If verified, we will publish it on the Exam News page promptly.
    How frequently is content updated?
    Exam notifications are updated as official announcements are released by GSSB, GSHSEB, NTA, and CBSE. Practice questions are reviewed and expanded periodically. Community suggestions are the primary driver of content additions.
