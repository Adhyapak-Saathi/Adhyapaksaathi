TAT Secondary 2025 — Detailed Notification Released by GSSB Gujarat

GSSB has released the official notification for TAT (Secondary) 2025. The examination will be conducted for Secondary school teacher posts (Class 9–10). Eligible candidates holding a B.Ed. degree may apply through the official GSSB portal. The exam pattern includes General Knowledge, Gujarati Language, and Subject Knowledge sections.
