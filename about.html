About Adhyapak Saathi

A free, academic preparation resource built by educators, for educators — dedicated to teacher exam aspirants across Gujarat and India.

Our Mission

Empowering Every Teacher
Aspirant in Gujarat & India

Adhyapak Saathi ("Teacher's Companion") was created with one purpose: to provide free, reliable, and curriculum-aligned preparation resources for those aspiring to serve India's classrooms as qualified teachers.

We focus exclusively on teacher eligibility and aptitude examinations — TAT (S), TAT (HS), TET I, TET II, UGC NET, GSET, and CTET — delivering practice questions, official syllabi, and timely exam notifications through a single accessible platform.

Our Principles

What We Stand For

🎯
Syllabus Accuracy
Every question is mapped to the official syllabus of the relevant examination — GSSB, GSHSEB, NTA, or CBSE. No irrelevant or inaccurate content.
📖
Academic Rigour
Questions and explanations are prepared with academic precision — cross-referenced with standard pedagogy textbooks and official guidelines.
🌐
Universal Access
Adhyapak Saathi is and will always remain free of cost — accessible on any device with an internet connection, without login or subscription.
🤝
Community Driven
Content is improved through feedback from real aspirants and practising teachers. Your suggestions directly shape what gets added next.
🔔
Timely Notifications
We monitor official portals (GSSB, GSHSEB, NTA, CBSE) daily and publish important exam updates, deadlines, and result links promptly.
📊
Reflective Practice
Detailed solutions after each test enable aspirants to understand concepts, not just memorise answers — promoting genuine learning.

Our Journey

How Adhyapak Saathi Grew

2022

The Beginning

Adhyapak Saathi started as a small collection of pedagogy MCQs shared among TET aspirants in a WhatsApp group in Ahmedabad.

2023

First Website

Launched as a static website covering TET I and TET II preparation with 150 questions across Child Development and Language sections.

2024

Expanded Coverage

Added TAT (S), TAT (HS), UGC NET, and GSET sections. Launched the Exam Notifications page to track official updates from GSSB, NTA and CBSE.

2025

Today — GitHub Pages

Redesigned as a fully professional academic portal with 500+ questions, 7 exam categories, and a community suggestion system. Now hosted on GitHub Pages for reliability.
