About Adhyapak Saathi

A free, one-stop academic preparation resource built by educators, for educators — dedicated to teacher exam aspirants across Gujarat and India.

Our Mission

The Ultimate One-Stop Solution for
Aspirants in Gujarat & India

Adhyapak Saathi ("Teacher's Companion") is your one-stop solution created with one purpose: to provide free, reliable, and curriculum-aligned preparation resources for those aspiring to serve India's classrooms as qualified teachers.

From live exam notifications and deep-dive practice tests to comprehensive study materials, we deliver everything you need to succeed in TAT (S), TAT (HS), TET I, TET II, UGC NET, GSET, and CTET — all through a single, accessible platform.

Our Principles

What We Stand For

🎯
Syllabus Accuracy
Every question is mapped to the official syllabus of the relevant examination — GSSB, GSHSEB, NTA, or CBSE. No irrelevant or inaccurate content.
📖
Academic Rigour
Questions and explanations are prepared with academic precision — cross-referenced with standard pedagogy textbooks and official guidelines.
🌐
Universal Access
Adhyapak Saathi is and will always remain free of cost — accessible on any device with an internet connection, without login or subscription.
🤝
Community Driven
Content is improved through feedback from real aspirants and practising teachers. Your suggestions directly shape what gets added next.
🔔
Timely Notifications
We monitor official portals (GSSB, GSHSEB, NTA, CBSE) daily and publish important exam updates, deadlines, and result links promptly.
📚
All Academic Solutions
From previous year question papers (PYQs) to syllabus PDFs and GCERT/NCERT study materials, find everything in one convenient place.
