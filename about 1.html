What is Adhyapak Saathi?

Your Free Companion for
Teacher Eligibility Examinations

Adhyapak Saathi (meaning "Teacher's Companion") is a free, open-access academic preparation portal built for individuals preparing for teacher eligibility and aptitude examinations in Gujarat and across India.

The platform provides practice tests, exam notifications, official circular downloads, and study resources — all in one place — without any registration, subscription, or cost.

This website is designed for aspirants of TAT (S), TAT (HS), TET I, TET II, UGC NET, GSET, and CTET examinations. All content is aligned with official syllabi published by the respective conducting bodies.
