TET I & II · Gujarat
TET Exam Guidelines & Syllabi
Primary and Upper Primary teacher eligibility tests (TET 1 & 2) are conducted by the SEB. Aspirants must download the official exam structure directly from the SEB portal to avoid misinformation and fake syllabi online.
Check SEB Portal →
UGC NET · National
UGC NET Notifications by NTA
The National Testing Agency (NTA) conducts the UGC NET exam bi-annually for Assistant Professor and JRF roles. Keep track of the application windows, city intimation slips, and result declarations on the official NTA website.
Visit NTA Portal →
CTET · CBSE
Central Teacher Eligibility Test (CTET)
The CBSE manages the CTET for teaching positions in central government schools (KVS, NVS). Access the information bulletin, submit applications, and download admit cards exclusively via the CTET official web portal.
Visit CTET Portal →

⚠️ Beware of Fake News

Social media is often flooded with fake exam dates and unofficial syllabus changes. Adhyapak Saathi strongly advises all teacher aspirants to only trust notifications published on official government websites.

Always verify news by clicking the official links provided in our portal.
