TAT (S) · Gujarat 📅 February 2025
TAT Secondary 2025 — Detailed Notification Released by GSSB Gujarat

The Gujarat Secondary Service Board (GSSB) has released the comprehensive official notification for the Teacher Aptitude Test (Secondary) 2025. The examination will be conducted for Secondary school teacher posts (Class 9–10). Eligible candidates holding a B.Ed. degree with relevant subject graduation may apply through the official GSSB portal. The exam pattern includes General Knowledge, Gujarati Language, and Subject Knowledge sections. Candidates are advised to refer to the official syllabus available on gssb.gujarat.gov.in.

Saurashtra University has published a tentative schedule for GSET 2025. This eligibility test qualifies candidates for Assistant Professor posts in Gujarat colleges.

TAT (Higher Secondary) 2025 — Exam Date and Pattern Announced
GSSB Gujarat has announced the exam schedule for TAT (HS) 2025. The exam pattern includes 150 MCQs covering Subject Knowledge and General Studies.
